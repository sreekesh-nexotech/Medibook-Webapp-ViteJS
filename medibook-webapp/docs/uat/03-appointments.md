### Faults found (NEW unless an existing blocker ID is cited)

- F1. Zero-total walk-ins are stuck. `ck_doctor_fee` allows ₹0 and `follow_up_fee_paise` may be 0 (free follow-up), so `total_paise` can be 0 with `payment_status=unpaid`. Backend collection needs every line ≥1 paise summing to the due, so ₹0 can never be "paid". The FE treats it as Pending Payment: it shows "Collect ₹0", its modal refuses ("greater than zero"), and Check in stays hidden until paid. The booked modal skips the call but marks the row "Paid" locally and offers a Receipt that 404s. Evidence: `appointments.view.ts:121-129`, `AppointmentPaymentModal.tsx:90-93`, `AppointmentBookedModal.tsx:81-88,128,144-153`; `payments/services/desk.py:21-46`, `catalog/models/doctor.py:35-36,63`, `appointments/services/booking.py:282`. Impact: a free follow-up cannot be checked in from Appointments. Fix (both): the backend sets `payment_status=paid` (or a new `not_required`) when `total_paise=0` at booking; the FE treats `totalRupees===0` as nothing to collect.
- F2. Stale caches after desk actions. Collect, refund, cancel and reject do not invalidate `['payments']` (payments page, totals, cash summary, open-drawer expected cash; 30 s stale time). Check-in, no-show, cancel and booking do not invalidate `['token-queue']` sessions, and nothing invalidates `['dashboard']`.
